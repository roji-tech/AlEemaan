## 1. What exists (read from the code and the screens, 2026-11-18)

- **Pupil:** the form asks for first name, last name and gender; the admission number (generated for the year when blank), date of birth, class and arm, guardian name, phone and e-mail are optional. A pupil is in one branch (`StudentRecord.branchId`).
- **Staff:** `StaffRecord.branchId` is required, and its optional link to a sign-in account is the pair (`userId`, `branchId`) to a `Membership`. A record never creates a sign-in. Category is Teaching or Non-teaching (`StaffCategory`).
- **Does adding a staff record create an account? No.** The Staff screen says "A record does not create a login", each row shows "Account: No sign-in" until a member is linked (Manage > Link to a member), and the service never creates a user. An account exists only through an invitation (Users > Invite someone: one e-mail typed at a time, or Invite many from a file). The two are not connected: inviting a person does not link them to their staff record, and a staff record gives no way to invite.
- **Departments: none yet.** A staff record has a free-text `designation` and its subject-and-arm assignments (`StaffSubjectAssignment`); nothing groups staff, and no one is a head of anything.
- **Invitation:** `email`, `role`, `branchId`, `tokenHash` (unique, required) and `expiresAt` (required): one step creates the row and e-mails the link.
- **School details:** one row (`SchoolSettings`, id `global`): name, principal, phone, motto, address, e-mail, website. Used by report cards, certificates, invoices, invitation e-mails, the landing site's contact page and the brand helper. The per-branch settings mechanism already exists for other settings (`SettingScope.BRANCH` plus an audit row), but this one is school-wide only.

## 2. Proposed design

- **Requirement R1 (the maintainer): one teacher may teach in two or more branches.** Today that is already possible and is done with one staff record per branch (a record has one `branchId`, and its account link is the pair `userId` + `branchId` to that branch's `Membership`; task 0092 lets one person hold a membership in several branches and be invited to several in one e-mail). What is missing is that nothing says two such records are **the same person**: names, phone and e-mail are typed twice and drift apart, the Staff list shows the person twice with no hint, and "invite" would send two e-mails. Proposed (the small change, no rebuild of the staff model): a nullable `StaffRecord.personId` (a shared id, generated for a person's first record) ties a person's records together. A row action **"Also works in another branch"** (superadmin, or an administrator of both branches) creates the second record in the chosen branch, prefilled with the person's details and the same `personId`; the list shows a fact "Also in: Secondary (Arabic)" on each of the person's records; editing the personal details (names, phone, e-mail, date of birth, qualification, corps member) offers "Update in the other branches too"; everything that belongs to a branch stays on that branch's record: category, designation, subject and arm assignments, standing, departments, drafts. A person's records are never merged automatically by a matching e-mail (two different people can share a school address). The larger alternative, splitting a school-level person from per-branch placements, is cleaner and is a data migration of every staff screen: not proposed unless the maintainer wants it.
- **D1. A draft pupil or draft staff record** is a normal record with `isDraft = true`, saved with only a first and a last name. It is listed with a "Draft" badge and a line saying what is missing ("Missing: gender, class"), has a Drafts filter, and an action "Complete" that checks the full set of required fields and clears the flag. While a draft it is **left out of everything that counts people**: class lists and arm counts, attendance, invoicing, promotion, report cards, program places, the portal sign-in, bulk "sign everyone in". Rejected alternative: no flag, just relax the required fields. Nothing would then tell the office which records are unfinished. *(ask: what must be filled to complete a pupil: gender and a class? to complete a staff record: category and a branch?)*
- **D2. A draft invitation** is an `Invitation` that has not been sent: `sentAt` (nullable); the token and the expiry are made when it is sent, not when it is saved (`tokenHash` and `expiresAt` become nullable). Drafts are listed under Pending invitations with a "Draft" badge, each with Send and Edit, and a "Send all drafts" button; the bulk invitation file gets a "Save as drafts, send later" choice. A draft can be deleted (an invitation that was never sent has no link to revoke). It expires only after it has been sent.
- **D3. Staff with no branch yet** (`StaffRecord.branchId` becomes nullable): the school's own list of people who are not placed. Only a superadmin sees and edits them (a branch administrator has no authority over a record that belongs to no branch). "Place in a branch" sets the branch (and from then on the branch's administrators manage the record). Until placed, a record cannot be given classes, subjects or an account link, because all of those belong to a branch. The bulk staff file may leave the branch out. Pupils stay in a branch: not asked for. **R1 applies: placing an unplaced record in a branch uses the record; "Also works in another branch" then adds the others** (a person is placeable in as many branches as they work in).
- **D4. School details per branch:** a new table `BranchProfile` (`branchId` unique; principal, phone, motto, address, e-mail, website, all nullable). What a report card, certificate, invoice or invitation e-mail shows for a branch is the branch's value when it has one, else the school's (`profileFor(branchId)` in one place, so no screen decides for itself). The school name stays school-wide (a branch already has its own name). The public website's contact page stays the school-wide record. Settings > School Profile gets the branch bar: "School-wide" and each branch, each field showing "Using the school's" until it is overridden; every change is audited. *(ask: may a branch administrator edit their own branch's details, or superadmin only?)*
- **D5. NYSC corps member:** *(built in task 0111, 2026-11-19: the column, the form tick, the badge, the filter and the bulk column; the "Update in the other branches too" part waits for R1.)* `StaffRecord.corpsMember` (boolean, default false), a checkbox "NYSC corps member" on the staff form, a badge "Corps member" in the list, a filter, and a "Corps member" column (yes/no) in the bulk staff file. It is a fact about the person, so under R1 it is one of the details offered for "Update in the other branches too". *(ask: also keep the service end date, so the office is reminded when a corps member's year is ending?)*

- **D6. Invite staff from the staff list, one or many.** Each staff row gets "Invite to sign in" (for a record with an e-mail address and no account); the list gets a selection mode: a checkbox on each row or card and a bar "N selected: Invite to sign in, Clear" (a kit feature, `Item` takes a `select` prop, so Pupils and Users can use it later). Inviting one or several opens the existing bulk invitation review **prefilled** from the records (e-mail, the role from the kind: Teaching staff or Non-teaching staff, the record's branch), so a record with no e-mail address shows as a problem row to fix, a wrong address is caught by the same checks as a typed one, and a large selection is saved in batches with the progress tray. The invitation carries the staff record (`Invitation.staffRecordId`, nullable), so **accepting it links the new account to that record by itself** (no separate Manage > Link step); an address that already has an account is linked to the record at once, with the same confirmation as the manual link. The Users page's "Invite someone" dialog also gets "From staff records": the same multi-select of unlinked records. Every invitation is still one e-mail to the person, and the record's e-mail is the only address used. **R1: selected records that share a `personId` make ONE invitation naming all their branches** (the multi-branch invitation of task 0092), one e-mail for the teacher, and accepting it links the account to each of those records. *(ask: which role should "Teaching" and "Non-teaching" records get? Teaching staff and Non-teaching staff, as above; an administrator is always chosen by hand.)*

- **D7. Departments, optional, and heads of department.** A branch that wants them turns on "Use departments" (a branch setting, off by default, so a school that does not use departments sees nothing new). Then: a `Department` is a branch's own named group (Sciences, Languages, Arabic and Islamic Studies, Bursary; unique by name within the branch, archived and never deleted like everything else here, teaching or non-teaching). A staff record can belong to several departments (a teacher of Chemistry and of Arabic), through `DepartmentMember` (department, staff record, `isHead`). **R1: departments belong to a branch and membership is on the branch's record, so a teacher can be head of Sciences in one branch and an ordinary member of Languages in another.** **A head of department** is a member marked as head; a department has one head (the choice replaces the old one, with a confirmation and an audit row) unless the maintainer wants co-heads. Subjects may be put in a department (`Subject.departmentId`, nullable), so "Mathematics belongs to Sciences". On screen: Academics > Staff gets a Department filter, a department badge and an "HOD" badge on a record; a "Departments" tab lists each department with its head, members and subjects; assigning many people at once uses the same multi-select as D6 ("Add to department"); the bulk staff file gets a Department column. **What an HOD can do is the open question** *(ask)*: (a) nothing yet, it is a title that shows on the list, the department page and, later, printed documents; (b) the head sees and edits their department's members and subjects; (c) the head approves the marks of their department's subjects through the existing results-approval flow (`CAN_APPROVE_RESULTS`, scoped to the department's subjects) before the administrator's. Recommended: build (a) and (b) now, and (c) as its own task once the approval rules are agreed, because it changes who can publish results.

### D4 as built (task 0115, 2026-11-19): school details per branch

The (ask) of D4 is answered with the secure default, and every decision below is a default the maintainer may change.

**Who may edit.** A superadmin edits the school-wide values (as before, `PATCH /api/v1/settings/school`, superadmin only, unchanged) and the details of any branch. An administrator of a branch edits ONLY that branch's details. Teachers and everyone else: no access (404 for a branch that is not theirs to administer, 403 for a role that cannot administer).

**Where it is on screen.**
- Settings is still a superadmin page (the sidebar entry, the page and `tests/e2e/shell.spec.ts` / `users.spec.ts` all say so, and a branch administrator was never meant to open it), so it is NOT opened to branch administrators.
- Settings > School Profile: a chooser "Edit details for": School-wide (the form as it was, the default) or one branch.
- Approvals > Report cards (an administrator's page, with the branch's own report-card layout): a card "Details printed on <branch>'s report cards" with the branch chooser limited to the branches the person administers. That is where a branch administrator reaches it, next to the rest of what their report cards show.
- Both use one component, `BranchProfilePanel`: each field shows the branch's own value, or "Using the school's: <value>" in muted text with the field empty; a field is cleared back to the school's with "Use the school's"; saving sends only the fields that changed; a line says what the report cards will show.

**The table.** `BranchProfile` (`branchId` unique, cascade; principal, phone, motto, address, email, website, all nullable; created/updated). The school's name stays school-wide (a branch has its own name already). The public website's contact page stays the school-wide record (`lib/landing/contact.ts` is unchanged). Additive migration `20261119170000_branch_profile`, the maintainer's to run on production after the prod merge.

**One place decides.** `lib/settings/branch-profile.ts`: `mergeProfile(school, branch)` is pure (a branch value that is blank or only spaces counts as not set; each field says `source: "branch"` or `"school"`), and `profileFor(branchId)` reads the school row (never creating it) and the branch row and merges. A reader of details about ONE branch asks `profileFor(thatBranch)`; a document that spans branches or has none keeps the school-wide value.

**Readers.** Read from the code: the only things that print or send the six fields are the report card (`getReportCard`, then `ReportCard.tsx` and `report-card-export.ts`: principal, motto, address, phone). Certificates, invoices, invitation and broadcast e-mails and the public verification page use the school's NAME only, which stays school-wide, so they are not switched. Nothing prints the branch's e-mail address or website yet; they are kept so a branch can have its own and the next printed document can use them without another migration.

**API.** `GET /api/v1/settings/branch-profile?branchId=` answers `{ school, branch, effective }` (the school's values, the branch's own, and the merged value with its `source` per field). `PATCH` takes `{ branchId, ...fields }`, strict, with the same field rules and lengths as the school settings; an empty string or null clears that field's override. Only changed fields are written and only they are audited (`BRANCH_PROFILE_UPDATED`, before and after); a patch that changes nothing writes nothing. Rate limit: 30 saves per administrator per 5 minutes (the school settings route has none; this one is open to many more people).

**Files.** `prisma/schema/settings.prisma` (`BranchProfile`), `prisma/migrations/20261119170000_branch_profile`, `lib/settings/branch-profile-rules.ts` (pure) and `branch-profile.ts` (server), the shared field rules `contactFields` in `lib/settings/school-settings.ts` (the school's form and a branch's cannot drift), `app/api/v1/settings/branch-profile/route.ts`, `components/settings/BranchProfilePanel.tsx`, `SettingsView.tsx` (the chooser), `ApprovalsSections.tsx` (the card), `lib/academics/report-card.ts` (the one reader switched), the search index (School Profile and Report cards now answer "principal", "motto", "branch details").

**Tests written (task 0115).** Unit `tests/unit/branch-profile.spec.ts` (the merge rule, blank and whitespace, source flags, the preview line). API `tests/api/branch-profile.spec.ts` (who may: signed out, teacher, another branch's administrator, a superadmin; reading creates nothing; saving trims, names what changed and audits only that; saving the same value changes and audits nothing; clearing by empty, spaces or null; the school changing later; one branch never reaching another; the cascade; the school's rules refuse bad input; the rate limit; the report card shows the branch's value, the school's elsewhere, and the school's name always). Browser `tests/e2e/branch-profile.spec.ts` (Settings: choose a branch, "Using the school's", set, take back; a refused value shown by its field; Approvals: an administrator of one branch sees only theirs, of two chooses between exactly those two; axe in both themes; desktop and phone).

**Decisions beyond the brief.**
- A branch administrator reaches it from Approvals > Report cards, not Settings (Settings stays superadmin only everywhere). The school-wide route is unchanged.
- Saving a value equal to the school's still stores it as the branch's own (an explicit choice: it stays when the school's changes); "Use the school's" is how it is given back.
- The branch's e-mail and website are stored and editable but nothing prints them yet; the next printed document can use `profileFor` without another migration.
- The rate limit (30 saves per administrator per 5 minutes) is new for this route; the school's own route has none.

## 7. D1 as built: draft staff and draft pupils (batch 3a, 2026-11-19)

**Why now.** The maintainer, looking at Users > Staff > "New staff record" and "Add many": "how do I mark a new staff record or multiple records as draft?" There was no way: D1 was designed and nothing was built. This section is the design that was written BEFORE the code, then what was built.

**Decisions (the (ask) in D1 is answered here; the maintainer may change any of them):**
1. **The flag.** `StaffRecord.isDraft` and `StudentRecord.isDraft`, boolean, default false, with an index on `(branchId, isDraft)` each. One additive migration, `20261119160000_staff_pupil_drafts`. `StaffRecord.branchId` stays required (D3 is later) and invitations are untouched (D2 is later). A draft keeps its place in the branch.
2. **What a draft needs.** The same as a record today: a first and a last name, plus the kind for staff and the gender for pupils (the forms already require those). Everything else is optional.
3. **What "complete" means (the answer to the (ask)).** Pure functions in `src/lib/drafts/rules.ts`, unit-tested:
   - Staff: first name, last name, kind, **gender**, and **an e-mail or a phone**.
   - Pupil: first name, last name, gender, **a class** (an active enrolment) and **a guardian contact** (a guardian with a name and a phone or an e-mail). *(Changed on 2026-11-19, the maintainer: "LET'S GO WITH YOUR SUGGESTIONS": the date of birth is NOT asked, because the ordinary pupil form does not require it either (decision of 2026-11-18), so a draft cannot be held to more than a record. The guardian contact is read from the pupil's guardian LINKS (task 0113), the first linked guardian, main contact first, that has a name and a phone or an e-mail, else the first linked one; a guardian blocked from this pupil still counts as the family's contact, and an archived guardian does not.)*
   - `staffMissing(record)` and `pupilMissing(record)` return the labels of what is missing ("gender", "e-mail or phone", "class", "guardian name", "guardian phone or e-mail"); `missingText(labels)` is "Missing: gender, e-mail or phone". The server computes them and sends them with each record, so the screen never has a rule of its own.
4. **Marking.**
   - The staff form and the pupil form each get **Save as draft** next to Save, on a new record and on an edit. Saving as a draft a record that is already complete is allowed: it is the administrator's choice to hold it back. Plain Save on a draft keeps it a draft; plain Save on a new record makes a normal record (as today).
   - **Complete** is an action in the card's Actions menu and in the details view. It checks the rules above: an unfinished draft is refused with a 409 `INCOMPLETE` that lists what is missing; a complete one has its flag cleared.
   - **Mark as draft** is the opposite action for a normal record.
   - On the API: `isDraft: true` on create and on PATCH (a PATCH can set the flag but never clear it), and `POST /api/v1/staff/[id]/complete` and `POST /api/v1/students/[id]/complete` to clear it.
5. **Add many.** Both bulk dialogs (staff, pupils) get a checkbox before anything is read, **Save these as drafts**. The import endpoints take `asDraft: boolean` (default false). The rows' normal rules still apply (names, kind, gender, a class that exists); the completeness rules do not. The bulk audit entry carries `asDraft`; each row's own audit entry carries `isDraft`.
6. **Lists.** A "Draft" badge and a "Missing: ..." line on every card and row; a **Draft** filter (Any / Drafts only / Not drafts) in the staff Filters panel and in the pupil Filters panel; "N drafts" beside the count. Drafts are shown by default, with the badge, so nobody loses a record. The pupil filter is `draft=YES|NO` in `pupilFilterSchema`, read by `studentListWhere`, so the list and its export stay identical.
7. **Drafts are left out of everything that counts people or acts on them.** One predicate per model, used everywhere (`src/lib/drafts/live.ts`): `LIVE_PUPIL = { archivedAt: null, isDraft: false }` and `LIVE_STAFF = { archivedAt: null, isDraft: false }`.
   - **Pupils:** attendance roll and marking, result sheets, promotion preview, invoice generation, report-card ranking and class lists, communication recipients, the family sign-in (a draft pupil cannot be signed in as), the programs' pupil lists and places, the dashboard count and the class and arm counts. Drafts still appear in the Students list, its filters and its export when listed, and in the top-bar record search.
   - **Staff:** the invitation tick boxes and "Select everyone who can be invited", the invite API (skipped with the reason "Draft"), teaching assignments (409), the class-teacher choices, linking a sign-in account (409; the list of linkable accounts is empty), transfer (409), broadcast recipients and the programs' staff lists. Drafts still appear in the Staff list, its filters and its export, and in the top-bar record search.
8. **Audit.** `STAFF_DRAFT_SET` and `STAFF_COMPLETED`, `STUDENT_DRAFT_SET` and `STUDENT_COMPLETED`, each with the flag before and after. A record created as a draft says so in its `STAFF_CREATED` / `STUDENT_CREATED` entry.
9. **Who.** Administrators of the record's branch, as for editing today. A teacher sees drafts read-only, exactly as they see records.

**Decisions made while building (beyond the list above):**
- **A draft holds nothing.** A record that has a sign-in, a waiting invitation, subjects, a class-teacher role or attendance access cannot be marked as a draft (409 with the reason), so none of those code paths ever meets a draft. A draft cannot be given any of them either.
- **A pupil's class can be set from the Edit form** when they have none. Without this a draft saved without a class could never be completed. For a pupil already in a class the field is ignored by the server (moving a pupil is Promotion's job).
- **Drafts are kept in the Staff and Students lists, their exports and the top-bar record search**, with the badge, so nobody loses one. They are out of everything that counts or acts.
- **Existing records are untouched:** the columns default to false, so every record that exists today stays a normal record.
