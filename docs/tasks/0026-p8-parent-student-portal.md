## 1. Overview & Objective

Slice P8 establishes a dedicated, unified, self-service portal (`/portal`) for parents, guardians, and students of AlEemaan School across all four branches (Primary English, Secondary English, Primary Arabic, Secondary Arabic).

Prior to this slice, staff and administrators had access to rich dashboards across all academic, attendance, results, finance, LMS, and messaging modules, while parents and students could only receive outbound notifications or view public verification tokens. Slice P8 provides parents and students with a frictionless, accessible, mobile-first portal to track academic progress, view published report cards, inspect attendance, view LMS coursework, and clear school fees online.

---

## 2. Architectural Guardrails & Security Model

1. **Strict Child/Parent Privacy (ADR 0012 Compliance):**
   - A portal user must NEVER access student records other than their own child/children.
   - Access to `/api/v1/students` remains restricted to staff; portal access uses dedicated `/api/v1/portal/*` endpoints that enforce child-ownership checks.
2. **Dual Access Paths:**
   - **Passwordless Magic Link:** Leverages `GuardianLinkToken` generated by the messaging engine (Slice P6) for frictionless 1-tap entry from SMS/WhatsApp/Email notifications.
   - **Student / Guardian Direct Verification:** Direct lookup via Admission Number (`admissionNo`, e.g. `2025/0001`) + Date of Birth / Guardian Phone verification for parents accessing without an inbound link.
3. **Multi-Child / Sibling Support:**
   - A guardian with multiple children across different classes or branches (e.g. child in Primary and child in Secondary) can seamlessly switch between children in a single unified view.
4. **Single-Pane-of-Glass Capabilities:**
   - **Results & Report Cards:** Published and locked term results with subject breakdowns, CA/exam components, grades, remarks, and class ranking.
   - **Finance & Fee Clearance:** Invoice breakdown, payment history, and direct Paystack payment link initialization.
   - **Attendance:** Term attendance summary, days present/absent, and roll-call logs.
   - **Coursework & Announcements:** View class assignments, teacher announcements, and learning resources.
5. **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA):**
   - High contrast ratios, semantic HTML, keyboard navigable tabs, mobile touch targets $\ge 44 \times 44$ px, 0 Axe violations.
