# School app — Pages

The signed-in school app: every page under the `(app)` route group of `src/app/`. They share one layout (`(app)/layout.tsx`: the session check, the shell with the sidebar and the
phone tab bar) and are listed here in the order of the navigation (`src/components/shell/nav.ts`, which is the single source of truth for who sees which entry). Hiding an entry is
a courtesy: each page and each API route behind it enforces its own access. The route has no route-group parentheses.

## Overview and account

| Route | File | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `layout (app shell)` | `(app)/layout.tsx` | Layout of the `(app)` route group: every signed-in page renders inside `AppShell`. It calls `requirePageSession()` (signed out goes to `/login`) only to show who is signed in and to pass `name`, `email`, `roleLabel`, `isAdmin`, `isSuperAdmin` and `isTeacher` (any active `TEACHING_STAFF` membership) to the shell. It is display only: a layout is not re-rendered when moving between its pages, so each page repeats the check itself. |
| `/dashboard` | `(app)/dashboard/page.tsx` | The Overview page; anyone signed in (`NAV` "Overview" has no flag), signed out goes to `/login`. A non-administrator gets "Welcome, name" and one tile per active membership (branch name and role label), or a note to ask an administrator for a branch. An administrator (any branch, or superadmin) gets three tiles (branch count, people with an active membership, own role) and up to six branches, oldest first, with active member counts. These counts are school-wide, not narrowed to the viewer's branches, and the "Manage branches" link is shown to every administrator although `/branches` is superadmin only. |
| `/account` | `(app)/account/page.tsx` | Anyone signed in (reached from the profile menu's "Profile" item; it is not in `NAV`); signed out goes to `/login` through `requirePageSession`. Server page (`force-dynamic`) that stacks cards: Profile (`ProfileDetails`, editable name), Your access (the caller's active memberships: branch name and role label), Email address (`ChangeEmailPanel`), Password (`ChangePasswordForm`), Two-step verification (`TwoStepPanel`, fed by `mfaConfigured()` and `getMfaStatus`) and Active sessions (`SessionsPanel`). |

## School set-up

| Route | File | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/branches` | `(app)/branches/page.tsx` | Superadmins only (`NAV`: `superAdminOnly`). Anyone else signed in sees an inline "You don't have access to this page" alert with a "Back to overview" link to `/dashboard` (no redirect, and no branch data is fetched for them); signed out goes to `/login`. A superadmin gets every branch, oldest first, with its count of active (not deactivated) memberships, and the colocated `BranchesView` renders them. |
| `/settings` | `(app)/settings/page.tsx` | Superadmins only (`NAV` `superAdminOnly`). Anyone else signed in sees an inline "You don't have access to this page" alert with a "Back to overview" link to `/dashboard`, and nothing is loaded; signed out goes to `/login`. A superadmin gets the school profile (`getSchoolSettings`, unset fields as empty strings), every branch, and every non-archived academic session with its non-archived periods (`listSessions`, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`), rendered by the colocated `SettingsView` with whether the superadmin has two-step verification on (for the "Results approval" tab's confirmation dialog). A Discipline tab: may branch administrators suspend (task 0045), the shared ceiling-switch screen. |
| `/academics` | `(app)/academics/page.tsx` | Classes and subjects, assessment schemes and grade scales, staff and promotion, as four tabs (`AcademicsSections`). Anyone signed in may load it (signed out: redirect to `/login`); the nav lists it for administrators only (`adminOnly`) but the page does not check the role. It offers the branches where the person holds a staff role (a superadmin: all) and sets `canEdit` only where they administer the branch, so a teacher who types the URL gets a read-only view. With no staff branch it shows "You don't belong to a branch with staff access yet." The Staff list shows a standing badge and the Suspend / Terminate / Reinstate / History buttons; a terminated record is under Archived and offers Reinstate, not Restore (task 0045). |
| `/users` | `(app)/users/page.tsx` | Administrators (an `ADMIN` membership in any branch, or a superadmin; `NAV` `adminOnly`). Anyone else signed in sees an inline "You don't have access to this page" alert with a "Back to overview" link to `/dashboard`, and no people are fetched; signed out goes to `/login`. An administrator gets the branches they may pick (all for a superadmin, their own `ADMIN` branches otherwise, from `adminScope`) and the `UsersPanel`, given `currentUserId` and `isSuperAdmin`. A suspended or terminated person shows a standing badge and Lift suspension or Reinstate instead of Reactivate; Suspend, Terminate and History buttons for those who may (task 0045). |
| `/approvals` | `(app)/approvals/page.tsx` | Approvals and rules: requests waiting for a decision, report-card layout, what the public verification page shows, and (superadmin only) who may approve their own marks. For administrators (`adminOnly` in `NAV`); anyone else who types the address sees an inline "Only administrators can open this page." with no redirect (a signed-out visitor goes to `/login`). Lists every branch where the caller holds a staff role, each flagged `canEdit` only when they administer it, and renders `ApprovalsSections`. `force-dynamic`. |
| `/question-bank` | `(app)/question-bank/page.tsx` | Staff (an active `ADMIN`, `TEACHING_STAFF` or `NON_TEACHING_STAFF` membership, or a superadmin; `NAV` `staffOnly`). Anyone else signed in sees a "Only staff can open this page." alert; signed out goes to `/login`. `QuestionBankSections` gets `writeBranches` (the branches the person is staff in, all for a superadmin: where they may write), `canReview` (a superadmin, a branch administrator, or anyone with a reviewer row), `isAdmin` and `isSuperAdmin`, and, for an administrator, `branches` (those `branchIdsFor` returns, each flagged `canEdit` only where `mayAdminister` is true). Everyone gets Browse and My questions; a reviewer also the Review queue, Reports (the reports on questions, answered there) and the Print log; an administrator also Reviewers and Settings. On an approved question someone else wrote, a teacher can "Report a problem". Any question can be printed ("Print"), and a reviewer or administrator also gets the Print log. The header text differs for an administrator and other staff. |
| `/forms` | `(app)/forms/page.tsx` | Staff (an active `ADMIN`, `TEACHING_STAFF` or `NON_TEACHING_STAFF` membership, or a superadmin). The `NAV` entry is `staffOnly` (administrators and teachers), so non-teaching staff reach the page, and its "For you" section, by its address. Anyone else signed in sees an "Only staff can open this page." alert; signed out goes to `/login`. `FormsSections` gets the branches the person is staff in (all branches for a superadmin): the sections Practice sets, Questionnaires, Paper work (write, publish, close) Marking (the attempts of practice sets to mark, across the branch's forms) and For you (answer the questionnaires addressed to you). A public questionnaire's link points at `/q/{token}`, which is built in slice F5. Display only: the forms APIs decide. |
| `/forms/[id]` | `(app)/forms/[id]/page.tsx` | One form (Task 0041, slice F2a). Staff of the form's branch (an active `ADMIN`, `TEACHING_STAFF` or `NON_TEACHING_STAFF` membership there, or a superadmin); a pupil or guardian sees "Only staff can open this page."; an unknown id, a malformed one or a form of a branch where the person is not staff all show "We could not find that form." with a link back to Forms, so nothing tells the cases apart; signed out goes to `/login`. The page only finds the form's branch and kind; `FormPage` asks the APIs for the rest and they decide who may see or change it. Tabs Overview (details, edit, publish, close, print), Questions (the answer key only where the API sent it), Results (a practice set's responses, a questionnaire's summary), Marking (a practice set that has a question marked by hand: that form's attempts to mark) and, for paper work, Roll call (who handed in, who was late, notes, sync the class). Reached from the title of a row in `/forms`. |
| `/programs` | `(app)/programs/page.tsx` | Every member of staff (an active `ADMIN`, `TEACHING_STAFF` or `NON_TEACHING_STAFF` membership, or a superadmin): the `NAV` entry is `allStaff`, in the Classroom section and the phone's More sheet (task 0043, slice P1). The page lists the programs the person has a place in: all for a superadmin; the ones they run as a program admin; the ones that run in a branch they administer. A teacher with no place sees "You are not part of any program yet." The New program button is a superadmin's. Display only: `GET /api/v1/programs` decides what is listed. |
| `/programs/[id]` | `(app)/programs/[id]/page.tsx` | One program (task 0043, slice P1). `viewerOf` runs first: a person with no place in the program (not a superadmin, not its program admin, not an administrator of a branch it runs in) is told "We could not find that program.", word for word what an id that does not exist gets (a sentence and not `notFound()`: the app has no not-found page of its own and Next's default one carries an inline style the CSP refuses). Overview, Structure, People (slice P2: everyone in the program, one place each; a branch administrator sees only the people through their own branch) and Staff (slice P3: program admins, supervisors, teachers and recorders, and the groups a teacher or a recorder takes attendance for) tabs, then Attendance (the roll, the summary, the week; shown only while attendance is on and the person reaches a group) and Settings (the program's own attendance switch and threshold; a superadmin and a program admin); what each button does is decided by the `can` and `attendance` objects the API returns, and every action is checked again on the server. Slices P3b to P5 add the tabs Assessments, Fees and Completion (a branch administrator sees Certificates in place of Completion). |
| `/programs/[id]/certificates/[certId]` | `(app)/programs/[id]/certificates/[certId]/page.tsx` | A super admin, a program admin, or a branch administrator for a person through their branch (the page asks `GET .../certificates/[certId]` and shows "We could not find that certificate." otherwise) | The print-ready certificate (task 0043 slice P5): the school's look, the wording and signatories as they were when it was issued, and a QR code to `/verify/[token]` drawn in the browser. Printed from the browser; a withdrawn certificate is not printable. |

## Teaching and records

| Route | File | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/students` | `(app)/students/page.tsx` | The pupil register of each branch (`StudentsPanel`): search, register, edit, archive and restore, CSV import. Anyone signed in may load it (signed out: redirect to `/login`); the nav shows it to administrators and teachers (`staffOnly`). It offers the branches where the person holds a staff role (a superadmin: all) and sets `canEdit` only where they administer the branch; the students API decides every read and change. With no staff branch it shows "You don't belong to a branch with staff access yet." Each pupil shows a standing badge and, to a viewer who may, Suspend / Lift suspension / Expel / Reinstate / History buttons (task 0045); the Show filter has Suspended and Expelled. |
| `/lms` | `(app)/lms/page.tsx` | Coursework page (assignments, study materials, quizzes). Its `NAV` entry is `staffOnly` (administrators and teachers) but the page only needs a session, so a student or parent who types `/lms` gets it with no staff buttons; a signed-out visitor goes to `/login`. Branches: all for a superadmin, otherwise those of the caller's memberships. Builds `rolesByBranch` (the role held in each branch, ADMIN everywhere for a superadmin) and renders `LmsPanel`, or a notice when there is no branch. `force-dynamic`. |
| `/attendance` | `(app)/attendance/page.tsx` | The daily roll call (`AttendancePanel`). Anyone signed in may load it (signed out: redirect to `/login`); the nav shows it to administrators and teachers (`staffOnly`). It offers the branches where the person holds a staff role (a superadmin: all) and passes `isAdmin` (administers at least one branch, or superadmin), which adds the "Manage Permissions" dialog. Which arms a person may take is decided by the attendance API, not here. With no staff branch it shows "You don't belong to a branch with staff access yet." A suspended pupil stays on the roll with a Suspended badge; an expelled pupil is not on it (task 0045). |
| `/results` | `(app)/results/page.tsx` | Entering, reviewing and publishing marks and reading report cards, as tabs (`ResultsSections`). Anyone signed in may load it (signed out: redirect to `/login`); the nav shows it to administrators and teachers (`staffOnly`). It offers the branches where the person holds a staff role (a superadmin: all), marks each `canEdit` where they administer it, and passes `isAdmin` (administers any branch, or superadmin), which adds the Review tab. The header is hidden when printing. With no staff branch it shows "You don't belong to a branch with staff access yet." |
| `/timetable` | `(app)/timetable/page.tsx` | The weekly timetable (`TimetablePanel`, "Timetable & Scheduling"). Anyone signed in may load it (signed out: redirect to `/login`); the nav shows it to administrators and teachers (`staffOnly`). It offers the branches where the person holds a staff role (a superadmin: all) and passes `isAdmin` (administers at least one branch, or superadmin) as the only edit switch, so the controls are not decided per branch; the timetable API refuses a change in a branch the person does not administer. With no staff branch it shows "You don't belong to a branch with timetable access yet." |

## Communication and money

| Route | File | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/announcements` | `(app)/announcements/page.tsx` | Announcements and broadcasts feed. Open to anyone signed in: no role check here and no flag on its `NAV` entry; a signed-out visitor goes to `/login` (`requirePageSession`). Loads every branch's id and name for the filter and renders `AnnouncementsPanel`; `canCreate` is true for administrators, teachers and superadmins, and `userBranchId` is the first membership's branch. `force-dynamic`. |
| `/communication` | `(app)/communication/page.tsx` | Communication page: broadcasts, dispatch logs and notification templates. For administrators (`adminOnly` in `NAV`); anyone else who types the address sees an inline "Only administrators can open this page." with no redirect (a signed-out visitor goes to `/login`). Branches: all for a superadmin, otherwise those of the caller's memberships. Renders `CommunicationPanel`, or a notice when the caller has no branch. `force-dynamic`. |
| `/finance` | `(app)/finance/page.tsx` | Finance and fee invoicing page. For administrators (`adminOnly` in `NAV`); anyone else who types the address sees an inline "You don't have access to this page" card with a link back to `/dashboard`, with no redirect (a signed-out visitor goes to `/login`). Branches: all for a superadmin, otherwise the ones the caller administers (`adminScope`). Renders `FinancePanel`, or a notice when there is no branch. `force-dynamic`. A Payment Rules tab for the part-payment rule, and the invoice says what may be paid and can be marked pay in full (task 0046). |
